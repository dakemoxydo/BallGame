## Rebirth progression

Rebirths require level 80. The level scale is balanced around accumulated Skill:
the first level-80 goal is 50,000 Skill; subsequent goals are
`floor(50000 * 1.6^rebirths)`: 80,000 / 128,000 / 204,800 and onward. Each level
threshold is `ceil(goal * ((level - 1) / 79)^2)`. Early levels arrive quickly,
then require progressively more Skill toward level 80. The original scale needed
66,439,530 Skill for the first level 80. Large rebirth counts have a finite
level-80 requirement capped at 1e15.

`Config.level(skill, rebirths)` searches the integer thresholds to avoid boundary
rounding mismatches. Both the HUD's level bar and server eligibility use the
current rebirth's scale. The rebirth window shows Level X / Level 80, and its
HUD percentage follows progress from level 1 to 80. Balls and pets accelerate
Skill earnings; equipping a stronger ball alone cannot skip levels. Shot power
and flight feedback retain their independent effective-power calculation.

Rebirth remains an explicit button action. It resets Skill and level to 1,
adds one rebirth, and retains currency, pets, equipment and worlds. Training is
released and blocked from immediately restarting on the same pad. Existing
saved Skill and rebirth counts are not automatically reset or converted.

- Hatch four types of eggs, singly or in batches of three. Store 60 pets, equip

- Claim daily rewards and six session-time gifts. Earn Auto Throw for 2,500 Wins.
- Unlock World 2 at the gate once for 100,000 Wins. It is a separate arena granting
  triple Skill and Wins. Eggs cost ten times as much and hatch pets with triple
  boost. Its portal returns to World 1; re-entry is free after unlocking. Respawning
  retains the current world.
- Adjust sound, music, camera shake, effects and HUD visibility.

### Streaming during long throws

`FlightStreaming` adds one server-controlled replication focus for the throwing
player, following `ShotMotion` two seconds ahead of the visual ball. The regular
character focus stays at the apron. The temporary focus updates five times per
second and is removed after the camera return, cancellation, death, respawn or
disconnect. It uses the authoritative shot parameters in both worlds; clients
cannot request arbitrary streaming locations.

This fixes scenery disappearing after Ember River on the expanded 2160-stud
route. Previously the camera left the character's streaming area: the server
contained all biomes, but the client had only 31 descendants of PolarExpedition
and 50 of SakuraThreshold, and Water_8 was absent.

Verified in Studio Play with StreamingEnabled: an actual 8700 m shot reached
sakura with the camera in Scriptable mode. Client counts matched the server
throughout the final biomes: MoonlightPromenade 1782, PolarExpedition 1733,
SakuraThreshold 2746, SakuraJapaneseFinale 940. The character stayed at the apron.
The focus disappeared after the throw. A World 2 throw used its own coordinates;
ending the admin test mid-flight removed the focus and restored original progress.
